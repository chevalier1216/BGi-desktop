# Asset Policy

## Current decision: M

- Phosphor MIT icons and Kenney CC0 assets are candidate or greybox-only sources.
- Do not download, import, purchase, or generate production assets without a later explicit decision.
- Existing Kenney Top-down Shooter assets may only support environment greyboxing, composition, and interface validation.
- Do not use those assets for the final skyline background, production decorations, characters, weapons, or product identity.

## Acquisition gate

- Before any production asset, external URL, paid asset, or generation service is used, record the current commercial terms, rights source, content limits, asset ledger fields, and intended use.
- Present the acquisition plan for user approval before import or generation.
